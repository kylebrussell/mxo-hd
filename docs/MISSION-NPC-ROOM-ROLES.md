Fresh NPC-only phases now derive coverage from the audited scene manifest rather than a sector switch. The first batch covers world 1 sectors 550, 842, 1150 and 1349. The existing 550/1349 payloads are unchanged. Other sectors retain legacy selection until exported and declared; missing/corrupt declared evidence refuses rather than silently using a legacy pose.

After the one ordinary site choice and measured entry, the planner resolves the site's exact audited GO19 identities to their own measured original marker positions (+95 standing height), preserving raw marker JSON and authored rotation, and checks original same-floor polygons, reciprocal portals and each required supported wooden door. It prefers the nearest authored room with enough distinct positions for ALL phase roles, including named NPCs and deferred waves. Authored role order and the existing marker ordering within a room remain stable. If no room has capacity, it expands through certified same-floor rooms. Facing variants do not create additional positions. No name, mission ID, arbitrary spacing radius or synthetic offset participates.

Selections persist `RolePlan: 1`. Existing selections without the field keep their original prefix validation and exact restore behavior. The new policy never reallocates fixed or pending actors on restore. Exact selected role, raw marker, standing position, scene route and door evidence remain validated before publication.
