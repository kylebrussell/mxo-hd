The shipped combat script returns zero from the ordinary damage calculation when the winner requested a special move. The separate `GetFullAbilityDamage` helper adds the damage bonus and any influence-scaled weapon contribution, then multiplies by the current tactic factor. The hash-pinned stock client constructs a reference to that helper at `62823843`, and `6258d910` marshals five arguments into the script call. The reviewed direct call and address references do not link that wrapper to either current ability's authoritative damage event. The shipped client also has a health modifier influence calculation at `625ec512–625ec58a`, which supports the current multiplier. Where the original server combined those operations for these two moves remains open. A useful original-server sample would identify the same landed ability at known rank and level, with known melee influence, resistance and tactic.
