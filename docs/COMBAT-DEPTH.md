# Combat depth

This implements target Determination mitigation, stealth-started opener damage, a floor-mesh visibility gate, named interlock specials and a shared NPC retaliation clock. These changes have gameplay effects; they do not establish complete retail combat parity.

## Evidence and limits

* `combat_calculations.py:GetAbilityMitigationDuration` is `duration * clamp(1 - max(0, Determination/5 - casterLevel)*.066, .33, 1)`. In the shipped client the sole call to wrapper `6258df10` is `625ec471`. Guards `625ec407..47c` require positive duration, on-use mask4, UDB0 debuff (`62515f30`) and unequal source/target locators (`625dfcc0`). The target getter `625c4e90` receives stat575. The server applies those guards to landed timed debuffs after caster duration bonuses, retaining float seconds. Immunity uses its original independent timer; control health-break rules remain intact. Buffs, self effects, zero/indefinite durations and immunity are excluded. Player575 comes from current capped buff/passive totals; NPC575 from its current timed totals. Complete character-attribute/NPC-template bases and the conversion of Determination Bonus1047 remain unknown and are not fabricated. P4 supplies ability1102's numeric toggle separately.
* `ability/spy/sneaks.py` stores bonus constants75/150/300/300/600/775/900/725/950/1200 for152/153/287/286/289/288/386/385/387/388. Its test functions are stubs and do not establish the engine's retail bonus trigger. **Server policy:** add the constant to the existing ability base when the cast began hidden (Sneak or Invisibility), before ordinary damage influence. Capture that boolean before `BreakForAction`; carry it through cast completion and NPC/PvP round queues. A failed/cancelled move consumes the cast snapshot. Ordinary use has no bonus. Existing base damage and retail damage reduction remain approximate.
* `ability/utility.py:SneakTest` and `InitialSneakTest` call `physics.clearLine` after the range/frustum tests. We lack the collision world. **Approximation:** `NavMesh.HasSightPath` samples the straight floor path every25 units, blocking mapped gaps or floor discontinuities. Unknown endpoint coverage preserves detection; it does not make unseen districts universally safe. This is not an eye-height raycast: a wall with continuous walkable floor, windows, thin geometry or airborne sight may differ. The gate applies to initial enemy watching, periodic player detection and NPC detection; it does not change stealth/disguise/blur bits or snapshots.
* `research/combat/extract_animations.py` reads the full ILDB resource0x24000b8b at PKB offset74324101/length9108938, assigns MOVE IDs in file order from255 (`62649920/62649a4a`), and exports all special-flag0x100 moves alongside existing tactic exchanges. Resource SHA256 `51caac6551d1b87cac1f1c4d2fffa820dc6053c7bc8e8c982a4971b903a04a4e`. `CombatAnimations.Special` maps61 ability IDs to their named choreography (aliases are explicit policy). Examples: HeadButt197 ->284/3440ms; CheapShot198 ->285/3066ms; Suplex287 ->19597/4166ms; SkyHighSidekick267 ->5937/2214ms. Participants and observers receive the same reference and source duration. Named failure moves are used where available, including failed Sneak variants. Style/tactic-specific variants use the corresponding name or a same-special Self-Defense fallback. Full ILDB rule evaluation, unmapped specials (notably NPC moves and several gun/throw attacks) and free-attack/out-of-interlock choreography remain gaps; no arbitrary numeric ability-to-animation conversion is used.
* **Shared-NPC policy:** retain separate pairwise interlock views while sharing the mob's health, combatant membership, retaliation/movement clock and death claim. Each NPC gets at most one action opportunity per own gun cadence or4500ms melee cadence across all attackers, including misses. Leaving/zoning one attacker preserves another target and the NPC's combat state; last departure clears it. HP read-modify-broadcast is serialized, and existing `TryClaimDeath` still admits one death/reward. This does not implement a single retail six-slot NPC group handler or XP sharing. Taunt still chooses its active taunter; otherwise whichever eligible session obtains the shared action opportunity is attacked.
