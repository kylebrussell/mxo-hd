# Public street-to-mission entry

This package adds a separate audited original outdoor-access layer. Fresh world1 interior-content stages require a reciprocal original outdoor-network component, a counted public opening and an own-GO native door, then an existing validated interior path to the lobby. It is not an all-world navigation or collision proof. Doorless openings without positive original open semantics remain refused.

A public door response keeps the original static key and raw CSV record, selects its original native GO before creation, and uses its measured pose. Ordinary public Open never rides to a mission floor. Request output is tied to the captured client/world/life/transport lifetime. Mission floor/exit waypoints remain persisted car points; public approach/return markers are presentation only.

New missions enroll explicitly. Old current, pending and fixed checkpoints do not acquire street metadata or new selection filters. New pending choices retain their exact source/site/context. Malformed recognized street data quarantines the owned mission after item restoration. Ordinary bound/furniture stages use a source-choice carrier without manufacturing an NPC or free-object witness. Empty Upload/waypoint stages carry no pose authority.

## One-pass native check

1. Inspect `NativeQa --fixture-plan mission-public-street-access` (read-only), then use the normal discovered mode/reset workflow with CSR110. Keep the full PacketLog before cleanup.
2. Missions/Phone > Tyndall > Critical101213, accept normally. Only earlier receipts are provided. Record the actual selected site; do not force Mara/842 or retry a source/seed.
3. Outside, follow the public-door marker. Require a real Door/Open hover, normal Open, and ordinary walking through the opening into the lobby. Stop on brick, missing hover or collision.
4. Inside, require the marker to switch to the car. Use the ordinary elevator and verify the current mission actors are visible/reachable through supported ordinary doors.
5. On the authored exit, ride down and walk out through the public door. Check that a phase advance or completion does not remove ordinary world-door use.
