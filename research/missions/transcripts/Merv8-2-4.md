## Build notes (interpretation)
Story: Zion's red pill programs have revealed more Machine pod clusters, and the General, having sampled the power of the cluster by the Fields, is scouting further to estimate cluster density. Malphas explains the aim: count the humans in pods to estimate the Machines' total energy. The General's long-range signal strays and the Machines pick it up; the player clears them off and receives the broken transmission (clusters every 100 to 150 miles, a tenth the size of the one by the Fields). Sunshine, the Machines' former solar program, guesses that about 1/16th of the Earth's surface could hold pods. Malphas works it out to nearly 1000 clusters and close to 300 million humans; the Merovingian still finds the power figures don't add up, and starts to suspect why the Machines are not acting like their old selves.
