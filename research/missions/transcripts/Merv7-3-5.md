## Build notes (interpretation)
Story: the Elite Commando's kill-code attempt on the Oracle failed when the Kid took the bullet. The Merovingian, in a foul mood, wants a relay to the General outside the Matrix. The player sends the broadcast frequency over a low-band channel from a Merovingian data center (which the Machines then overrun), then sets an override script on a civilian media center's terminal to receive the General's broadcast. Watching it, the Merovingian has Malphas trigger override routines planted in the commando Sentinels, taking command of them.
