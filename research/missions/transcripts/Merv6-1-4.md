## Build notes (interpretation)
Story: the Merovingian's cheat codes are bringing the Twins back in pieces. The player takes the first Twin's torso and head from Mockingbird's hired Crushers, has the Effectuator join them into a floating Half Twin, leads it to its legs, leads both halves to the Effectuators, and gets one whole Twin.
