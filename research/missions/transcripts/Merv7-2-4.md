## Build notes (interpretation)
Story: the Apothecary is nearly done with the Oracle's kill-code, but Machines keep finding her. She won't work without security, so the player borrows two of the General's commando Elites through his hologram and leads them off to intercept the Machines, then goes to the facility where the cheat code's remains were kept to recover the vial, which is gone. The Machines reach the Apothecary again; once they are dead she declares the kill-code finished and leaves the simulation.
