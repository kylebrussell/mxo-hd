## Build notes (interpretation)
Story: the Machines took Mary MacHenry's body out of her pod by hand. The player gets data from a terminal that identifies the Machine caretaker program of her pod; with the Trainman gone, the Effectuator hijacks that program into a vampire's body so Malphas can question it, but it refuses and slips away. The player then takes the program's activity history from a Machine terminal; Persephone and Malphas read it: she was given heavy physical conditioning before she was unplugged, so the Machines wanted her able to function in the real world.

The log is complete apart from the top ("Mission Started!"), two possible one-line gaps in Flood's first briefing and possible entries at seven joins between images (after entries 1, 5, 8, 11, 18, 24, 27), so the mission is built.
