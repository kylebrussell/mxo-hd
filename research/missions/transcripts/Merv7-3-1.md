## Build notes (interpretation)
Story: with Sati rescued and his surface base lost, the General has fled into the Matrix with the Machines after him. Flood sends the player to him; the General hands over two of his commandos, Kaplan and Verdick, to hide, and Flood has the player get rid of them by leading them to the Machines. More Machines follow the player and are killed; the player then finds the General fighting Machines, clears them, and passes on the Merovingian's invitation. At the meeting the Merovingian tells the General he will be sent to a secure place near the surface, back in the Real; the General agrees.
