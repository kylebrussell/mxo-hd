## Build notes (interpretation)
Story: the Merovingian's mole on the Oracle's security detail was found out, but the kill-code was saved. Malphas
explains that the Keymaker's key, buried at Mahath Tower, will go with the kill-code to a new assassin; the General
(as a hologram) agrees to lend a squad of his commandos, but with the Trainman gone someone must run the transfer
program. Flood sends the player to take the program from the Trainman's workshop (its data was pulled off the
computer by one of the Club Guards), to the Effectuator, who botches it, and then to a computer in Tabor Park to run
the transfer while Machines attack. Four commandos made it.
