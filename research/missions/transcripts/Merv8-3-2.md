## Build notes (interpretation)

The Oracle answers only that the Machines "were simpler back then". Flood has the player take an access code to the Zero One archive from Binary Boy's computer; the Effectuator uses it to pull out a live runtime, the early robot Taskmaster, which the player brings to the Merovingian. Scanning its code, he concludes that the early Machines lacked complexity, and that this simplicity may have led to their reliance on humans.
