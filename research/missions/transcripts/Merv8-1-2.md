## Build notes (interpretation)
Story: Machine patrols in the tunnels threaten the General and the Merovingian's Sentinel fleet. Zion knows the maintenance tunnels better, so the Effectuator points the player to a Zion terminal in a business office, which he works on himself; it leads to a system in a building held by Zionite soldiers, where the player downloads the tunnel coordinates. The Merovingian uses them to steer the General's fleet (and confine it to areas he chooses, under the threat of an override). Flood, "emotionally divorced" from the mission, meditates through it ("Om...", "a sea of tranquility": the title).

The web summary in research/missions/web-missions.md ("4 areas: Malphas and the Effectuator; search computer, talk to the Effectuator; search computer past higher-level Zion enemies, then exit; talk to the Merovingian") agrees with this reading.
