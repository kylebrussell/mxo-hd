## Build notes (interpretation)

The player breaks into a Network Media broadcasting facility, opens a two-key security system and hijacks the Network's transceiver array from a third terminal. The General's second transmission reports another set of Fields over 6000 miles away, with power lines running away from the Machine city, and a dead, featureless world everywhere else; Machine signals close in as it ends. Malphas reckons 300 million humans in the pods and that the General must be called back; the Merovingian marvels at how little power that gives the Machines, and at the devastation outside.
