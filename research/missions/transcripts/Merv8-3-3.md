## Build notes (interpretation)
Story: the Merovingian is troubled that the Matrix's records show more births than deaths, which would not make a stable system, so Flood sends the player back to the Coroner, who had spoken of the Machines making people "disappear". The Coroner tells of Mary MacHenry, who died in a caving fall in South America, and of her last call to her mother: the tap of his dead friend Reynolds has her cancelling the trip because she felt poorly; her mother says she felt poorly but went anyway; the federal record has her feeling fine. The Coroner concludes that the records were changed and Reynolds killed to hide that Mary MacHenry did not die, and that "it goes far deeper". Flood dismisses the whole thing and passes it to the Merovingian under the player's name.
