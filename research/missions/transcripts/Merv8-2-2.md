## Build notes (interpretation)
Story: someone from E Pluribus Neo stole the General's scouts' maps of the Fields from the Merovingian, and ate his cake while doing it; the cake lets the Merovingian trace him. Flood has the player tip the Machines off with an anonymous call so they hit the EPN first. The Machines kill the thief (Druzhba) just as the player arrives, without the data; he reconstructs, takes RSI pills, and runs back to EPN headquarters, where the player kills him, but the data isn't there either. Flood, flustered, tells the player to keep away from the Merovingian.
