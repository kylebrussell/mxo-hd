## Build notes (interpretation)
Story: the General's projection passes on a Zion intercept: the hovercraft Ardeshir mentions an "emergency uplink" protecting Zion's broadcast control database from the Machines. Flood has the General's squids disarm the Ardeshir while the player clears Cellino's crew and makes her give up the uplink's coordinates. Malphas explains that the Merovingian sent Beirn to the uplink to save it, since a quick end to the new war would not suit him. The Merovingian finds the day "too easy" and wonders about the Machines; Beirn brags about slaying an Agent there.
