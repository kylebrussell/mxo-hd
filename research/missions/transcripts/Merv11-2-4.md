## Build notes (interpretation)
Story: the interface program has slipped into a human, Imelda Kroller. A Zionite heard Wright give the pod's serial number; the player takes the Zionite's access card and reads the activity report on a computer. The Merovingian plans to find the pod's caretaker, but Persephone insists on freeing the program herself. The player clears a Machine terminal and brings her to it; she frees it but is badly hacked by the Machines' defense routines.
